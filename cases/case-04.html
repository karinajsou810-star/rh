Trusted to act when things changed

Private executive office · Lisbon, Portugal

The challenge

Some situations cannot be anticipated or added to a calendar.

Executive support often means dealing with situations that cannot be anticipated or neatly added to a calendar.

In a high-trust environment, the expectation was not simply to follow instructions, but to use judgement, act independently and keep things moving when circumstances changed unexpectedly.

The approach

I acted, then reported. Not the other way around.

I operated as a trusted point of coordination across executive and operational matters, handling schedules, appointments, suppliers, staff, payments, documentation and time-sensitive logistics with discretion.

When an unexpected situation required immediate local action while key stakeholders were abroad, I assessed what needed to happen, coordinated with the appropriate people and handled the logistics through to resolution.

The result

A trusted point of execution when circumstances changed.

The people I supported could rely on matters being handled calmly, discreetly and without unnecessary escalation, even when they were not physically present to manage them themselves.
