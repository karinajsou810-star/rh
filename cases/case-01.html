The challenge

Volume was high, and every match still had to be the right one.

The Employment Centre of Cascais supported 1,000+ jobseekers and 40+ partner organisations while managing a high volume of vacancies, candidate profiles, interviews and employment documentation.

The challenge was keeping the process moving efficiently while making sure candidates were matched to opportunities that genuinely fit their experience and the employer's requirements.

The approach

I worked both sides of the hiring desk, end to end.

I worked across the hiring process from employer intake and requirements gathering through candidate screening, interview coordination, matching, referral and follow-up.

I reviewed candidate profiles against vacancy requirements, conducted screening interviews, coordinated communication between candidates and employers, and managed the documentation needed to move each placement forward.

I also proactively engaged with local organisations to understand their hiring needs and identify opportunities that matched the available talent pool.

The result

25% improvement in placement efficiency.

Supported 1,000+ jobseekers, worked across 240+ vacancies and coordinated with 40+ partner organisations, while conducting approximately five screening interviews per day.

The result was a more structured process for moving candidates from initial assessment to employer referral, with greater efficiency across a high-volume environment.
