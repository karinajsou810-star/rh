Recurring operations & peak-demand planning

Supporting an internationally based principal and coordinating recurring operations in Portugal.

Client context

An anonymised private executive office supporting an internationally based principal and coordinating recurring operations in Portugal.

Several times a year, the normal operating rhythm expanded to accommodate approximately 15-day visits involving the principal, family members and invited guests. These periods required a temporary increase in workforce coverage, procurement activity, supplier coordination and service capacity.

Operational challenge

The operation needed to move between normal, extended-visit and peak-demand conditions without compromising existing responsibilities.

Each high-demand period introduced multiple dependencies: changing schedules, day-to-night workforce handovers, procurement, supplier availability, individual dietary requirements and last-minute changes.

Because the principal was frequently based abroad, routine issues also needed to be resolved locally without creating unnecessary executive involvement.

Ownership & decisions

I assumed end-to-end ownership of planning and coordinating these recurring operating periods.

I assessed expected demand, determined workforce and procurement requirements, coordinated responsibilities between day and evening coverage and identified where advance preparation was necessary.

I also decided which exceptions could be resolved locally, which substitutions remained within established preferences and which situations genuinely required stakeholder approval.

This converted an unpredictable collection of requests into a manageable operational model with clearer capacity, contingency and escalation logic.

Solution & execution

I coordinated workforce schedules, handovers, procurement, supplier orders and changing stakeholder requirements throughout each 15-day period.

I checked available inventory before purchasing, anticipated recurring requirements and maintained alternative supplier options for time-sensitive items.

When individual requirements changed, I adapted the plan using available resources, alternative products or backup suppliers. Service-quality issues were addressed through immediate recovery actions rather than allowed to disrupt the wider operation.

During one peak period, the expected number of people changed approximately 30 minutes before service. I reassessed available capacity, redistributed responsibilities and coordinated preparation in parallel, allowing the additional demand to be absorbed within the remaining window.

Measurable outcome

Maintained operational continuity across recurring 15-day high-demand cycles involving near-daily procurement, changing stakeholder requirements and coordinated day-to-night coverage.

Scaled the operation to support approximately 14 people at peak and absorbed a critical late headcount change within 30 minutes.

The business value was not simply successful service delivery. It was the ability to scale operational capacity temporarily, manage exceptions locally and protect the principal from unnecessary day-to-day coordination.

Client relevance

Founder-led businesses, executive offices and growing teams frequently experience temporary demand surges around launches, events, travel, visiting stakeholders or seasonal activity.

This case demonstrates my ability to anticipate capacity requirements, coordinate interconnected resources and maintain execution when demand changes faster than the original plan.
