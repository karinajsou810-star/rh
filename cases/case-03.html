The challenge

Three processes, and none of them talked to each other.

Recruitment, onboarding, and scheduling ran side by side, each with its own steps and its own rhythm. With more than one thousand active candidates, every application was read one by one, every answer was written by hand, and candidates moved forward only when someone remembered to move them. There was effort everywhere, and clarity nowhere.

The people felt it on both sides. Recruiters spent their days on interviews that should never have been scheduled, while qualified candidates waited for a reply that took weeks. And the best candidates, the ones with more options, simply stopped waiting.

Deadlines slipped, allocation suffered, and the team worked harder every month to hold together a workflow that did not exist.

"We had three processes that didn't talk to each other: recruitment, onboarding, and scheduling. Candidates advanced through the process depending on someone pushing them manually."
Ana Barreiro · HR, Public Employment and Training Institute
The solution

One connected workflow, with people where they matter most.

The three processes were redesigned as one workflow, implemented step by step. Each application is now evaluated the moment it arrives. Candidates who do not meet the requirements receive a clear, respectful answer right away. Those who advance enter the pipeline already qualified, ready for interview, with no manual triage in between.

AI-assisted tools carry the repetitive work: reading applications, filtering profiles, sending updates. The team stepped into the work where human judgement is irreplaceable, evaluating borderline profiles, preparing candidates, and looking after the relationships that fill vacancies. The team doesn't do less work. They do different work.

And the candidate experience changed with it: an answer in hours instead of weeks, a clear next step at every stage, and a process that moves on its own, whoever is on holiday.

"The HR team doesn't do less work. They do different work. Candidates advance through the process without depending on someone pushing them manually."
Ana Barreiro · HR, Public Employment and Training Institute
