Client context

An anonymised private executive office relying on recurring and project-based service providers while the principal was frequently based abroad.

The wider operating environment included seven recurring third-party providers alongside additional specialists engaged for property setup, refurbishment and repair work. This case focuses on five documented service and project workstreams.

Operational challenge

Each provider operated with different responsibilities, schedules, commercial terms and service expectations.

Project-based requirements often began with incomplete information and needed to be converted into a workable scope before service providers could be sourced or instructed.

Without a central owner, the principal would have needed to manage quotations, approvals, access, follow-ups, documentation, quality problems and payment administration remotely.

Ownership & decisions

I became the accountable coordination point between the operation, external providers and the principal.

I clarified requirements, obtained quotations and determined which decisions could be handled locally and which required approval.

I also evaluated completed work against the agreed requirement. When delivery fell below expectations, I decided whether the issue required clarification, corrective work or stronger provider accountability before final acceptance.

This created a controlled service-provider lifecycle rather than a series of disconnected interactions.

Solution & execution

I coordinated the operational sequence from requirement through to completion:

requirement → scope → quotation → approval → scheduling → access → execution → quality review → corrective action → completion

For a new apartment setup, I translated broad requirements into sourcing, procurement, delivery and installation activities and coordinated execution with limited direct supervision.

I subsequently coordinated a three-bathroom refurbishment and several electrical and property repairs, maintaining communication between stakeholders and contractors throughout delivery.

When a contractor failed to meet the expected standard, I documented the gap, restated the agreed requirements and followed through until the necessary corrective work was completed.

Across seven recurring provider relationships, I also managed requests, schedules, service follow-up, supporting documentation and payment administration.

Measurable outcome

Established one accountable coordination point across seven recurring providers while also managing a new apartment setup, a three-bathroom refurbishment and related repairs.

Quality issues were identified and corrected before final acceptance, protecting the agreed scope and preventing incomplete work from becoming an unresolved operational problem.

Across the five documented workstreams, two missed attendance commitments were recovered and corrective rework was completed without an additional payment.

The commercial value was clearer accountability and verified closure: the principal retained approval and visibility while routine execution, provider follow-up and quality recovery were owned locally.

Client relevance

Growing businesses and executive offices often depend on multiple external providers without having a dedicated coordination function.

This case demonstrates my ability to translate ambiguous requirements into executable scopes, coordinate the service-provider lifecycle and maintain accountability through delivery, quality review and corrective action.
