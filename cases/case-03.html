Private executive office · Lisbon, Portugal

The challenge

Seven providers, seven sets of expectations, one operation.

The operation relied on 7 external service providers, each with different responsibilities, deadlines and service expectations.

The challenge was keeping everything aligned without requiring constant executive involvement, particularly when the principal was abroad.

The approach

I became the single point of coordination, and held the line.

I became the central point of coordination between the operation and its external providers.

I managed requests, clarified expectations, followed up on deadlines, verified completed work and coordinated payment administration.

When a supplier failed to meet the agreed terms, I addressed the issue directly, documented the expectations in writing and followed through until the original scope, price and deadline were respected.

The result

Issues resolved before they became operational problems.

Supplier issues were resolved before becoming larger operational problems, while deadlines, agreed pricing and service expectations remained protected.
