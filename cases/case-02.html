One day to five minutes: rethinking a recurring process

Private executive office · Lisbon, Portugal

~1 day → 5 min
Monthly leave and absence close
~90 hrs
Recurring admin work removed per year
1
Workflow, redesigned end to end
The challenge

A full working day, every month, on the same task.

A recurring monthly leave and absence process was taking approximately one full working day to complete, creating unnecessary administrative work and competing with higher-value priorities.

The approach

I redesigned the workflow instead of automating the symptoms.

I mapped the process from request to final close, identified repetitive manual steps and redesigned the workflow around structured inputs, approvals and automated actions.

Rather than automating isolated tasks, I built a more consistent workflow that reduced the need for manual intervention.

The result

From about one day to under five minutes.

The redesigned process eliminated approximately 90 hours of recurring administrative work per year, turning a time-consuming monthly task into a predictable and efficient workflow.
