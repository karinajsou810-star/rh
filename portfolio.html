What I stand for

My vision

I help leaders stay focused by bringing clarity, structure and follow-through to the moving pieces around them.

Good support is more than keeping a calendar updated or checking tasks off a list. It means understanding what matters, anticipating what could become a problem, connecting the right people and making sure commitments move from decision to done.

I work at the intersection of executive support, business operations and people coordination, bringing the judgement, discretion and operational ownership needed to keep things moving.

My values

Ownership. I take the problem and carry it through to resolution rather than passing it along.

Discretion. Trust is earned through consistency, sound judgement and knowing what should stay private.

Clarity. People should know what is happening, what matters and what comes next.

Anticipation. The best support happens before something becomes urgent.

Reliability. The standard stays high whether the day is predictable or everything changes at once.

Practicality. I look for simpler ways to get the work done without creating unnecessary complexity.
